Questions

Things people ask.

Does Relay replace Jira or Linear?

No. It connects to them. Relay is the shipping layer on top: it reads your issues and PRs and organises them into a cadence. Keep the tools your team already uses.

What does "release train" actually mean?

A train is a batch of work scheduled to ship together on a fixed date. Relay watches the train and warns you when it's likely to leave late.

Can we self-host it?

Yes, on the Enterprise plan. Run the whole app and runner inside your own infrastructure, with audit logs and SSO.

How is the changelog written?

From your merged pull requests. Relay drafts each entry and links it back to the PR, so the changelog stays honest without anyone writing it by hand.
