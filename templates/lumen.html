Questions

Do I need to know how to code?
Basic HTML helps, but the pages are plain and commented. Most buyers change text and colours, which needs no programming.
Can I use it for client work?
Yes. The licence covers unlimited personal and client projects. You may not resell the kit itself as a template.
How do I change the colour scheme?
Open lumen-assets/css/tokens.css and edit the theme block. Every page updates at once, no find-and-replace.
Does it work without a build step?
Yes. It is plain HTML and CSS. Open index.html in a browser and it runs. Deploy by uploading the folder.
