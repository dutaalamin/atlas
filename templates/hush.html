Features

Everything you need. Nothing that nags

Hush does a handful of things, and does them quietly. No dashboards, no engagement tricks.

Focus mode

One keystroke and the page fills the screen. Everything else fades out: toolbars, tabs, the world.

Writes offline

Your words live on your device. Write on a plane, in a tunnel, anywhere. It syncs when you're back.

Gentle history

Every draft is kept, without a timeline to obsess over. Restore any version in a click.

Quiet suggestions

Highlight a sentence and ask for a tighter one. It offers, never overwrites. Your voice stays yours.

Plain Markdown

Every note is a file you own. Take the whole folder anywhere. No lock-in, ever.

Kind by default

No streaks, no badges, no “you haven't written today.” Hush would rather you close it and live.

Questions

Is Hush really free?

Writing, focus mode and export are free forever, and work offline. An optional assistant for rewriting is included with a free monthly allowance.

Where are my notes stored?

In a folder you choose, as plain Markdown files. Nothing is locked in a proprietary format, and you can open them in any editor.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Hush is built offline-first. Everything works without a connection and syncs when you're back online.

Do I need an account?

No. Download it and write. An account is only needed if you want to sync across devices.
