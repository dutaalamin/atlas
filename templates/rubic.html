Questions

Is RUBIC really free?+

Yes. Playing, timing, scrambles and the leaderboard are free and work offline. Nothing is locked behind an account.

Do I need to install anything?+

No. It runs in any modern browser. You can optionally install it to your home screen for a full-screen, offline experience.

Which cube sizes are supported?+

2×2 through 5×5 today, with 6×6 and 7×7 in progress. Classic 3×3 is the default.

Does it work on my phone?+

Yes, touch gestures, drag-to-turn and a layout that adapts to small screens. Performance is tuned to hold 60fps on recent devices.

Can I use it without an internet connection?+

After the first load, everything is cached. Scrambles and timing keep working offline; the shared leaderboard syncs when you reconnect.
